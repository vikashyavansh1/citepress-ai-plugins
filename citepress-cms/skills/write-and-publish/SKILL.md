# Write and publish on a CitePress site

1. Ask what the piece is for and who reads it, if they have not said. Look at the site's existing
   content with the read tools first, so the new piece fits its style and does not repeat a post.
2. Draft with the site's draft tools; never publish straight away. Show the title, summary, headings and
   slug, and the draft's link on the site.
3. Edit until they are happy. Suggest internal links to related posts and a meta description.
4. Publish or schedule only when they say so. Tools that publish, delete or change many items at once ask
   for `confirm: true`; send it only after their yes.

If the site says its licence is needed for AI access, pass the message on as it is, link included.
